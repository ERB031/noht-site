Your Mission Deserves to Be Seen

Nonprofits change lives every day — but too often, that impact goes untold. At NØHT, we partner with mission-driven organizations to create cinematic, emotionally compelling content that captures what makes your work matter. Whether you need a fundraising film, event coverage, or a campaign that rallies your community, we bring the same professional craft we use for commercial clients — at rates designed for nonprofit budgets.

We're not a massive agency — and that's by design. When you work with NØHT, you get a dedicated, Atlanta-based team that genuinely cares about your cause.
